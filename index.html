Designing a Personalized Career Discovery App

A mobile-first executive job discovery platform using conversational UX and smart AI matching.

Experteer Beyond Jobs was a mobile app concept designed to help senior professionals find curated opportunities. Instead of uploading a CV, users answered guided questions that built their profile and visibility score.

Pain Points

  • Executives didn’t want to upload a résumé just to browse
  • Most job apps felt generic and lacked senior-level focus
  • No personalization in profile building or job discovery
  • Visual design didn’t reflect the premium target audience
  • New users didn’t understand how matching worked

Branding & Visual Direction

I created a brand system that reflected AI-driven personalization: Hexagon symbol for structure, dot-matrix "e" for digital identity, and green + black palette for contrast and elegance. Typography: Helvetica Neue. Background visuals were created in Photoshop.

Product Screens – Key User Flows

Onboarding

Animated intro explained the product vision and encouraged LinkedIn sign-in to simplify entry.

Profile Builder

Conversational flow replaced CV upload. Users answered questions and earned visibility points to unlock better job matches.

Login

Initial email/password version had high drop-off and was later simplified.

Premium Upsell

Subscription screen tested feature highlights and "bestseller" tags to improve conversion.

Dashboard

Users tracked their visibility score and were nudged to improve profile completion.

Job Feed

Curated job suggestions based on profile. Clear layout with company logos and recruiter links.

Outcome / Learnings

  • Launched app with real users and product data
  • Validated onboarding importance and trust-building
  • Learned that trendy UX (chat flow) didn’t fit all user mindsets
  • Simplified visuals improved frontend performance

Impact

  • +36% increase in user sign-up rate
  • +23% improvement in profile completion
  • +8% boost in upsell conversions

My Role & Process

I led all design efforts — branding, flows, UI, and onboarding motion. I collaborated closely with PM, engineers, and the founder. Tools: Sketch, Illustrator, After Effects, Photoshop.
